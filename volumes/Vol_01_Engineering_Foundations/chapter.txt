# 📘 Volume 01 — Engineering Foundations & Mindset

## 🎯 Objective

Build the mental models, problem-solving habits, research skills, debugging discipline, and engineering workflow required before learning Linux, networking, Python, cloud, Docker, AI, Kubernetes, and production infrastructure.

This volume is intentionally **tool-light**.

The goal is not to memorize DevOps terminology.

The goal is to learn **how to think and work like an engineer**.

By the end of this volume, you should be able to:

* Break unfamiliar technical problems into smaller problems.
* Distinguish symptoms from root causes.
* Think from first principles instead of memorizing solutions.
* Understand systems at a high level.
* Debug unfamiliar problems methodically.
* Read official documentation independently.
* Research technical topics without relying on tutorials.
* Form hypotheses and test them.
* Create minimal reproducible examples.
* Ask high-quality technical questions.
* Use AI as an engineering assistant without becoming dependent on it.
* Maintain useful technical notes and a learning journal.
* Work with Git and a structured engineering repository.
* Approach an unfamiliar technology without feeling lost.
* Explain technical concepts in your own words.

## Problem-Solving Framework

Use:

```text
1. What exactly is the problem?
2. What do I know?
3. What don't I know?
4. What are the constraints?
5. What are the possible causes?
6. What evidence do I have?
7. What can I test?
8. What result do I expect?
9. What actually happened?
10. What did I learn?
```

## Understanding Test

For every major concept, ask yourself:

```text
Can I explain it?
Can I draw it?
Can I implement a simple version?
Can I debug it?
Can I explain why it exists?
Can I explain when NOT to use it?
Can I answer "what happens underneath?"
```

If the answer is no, you probably don't fully understand it yet.
