# 📘 Volume 02 — Computer Architecture & Operating System Fundamentals

## 🎯 Objective

Understand what happens inside a computer from the moment it powers on to the moment a program executes.

This volume establishes the hardware and operating-system mental models required for the later DevOps and AI infrastructure volumes.

By the end of this volume, you should understand:

* How computers represent data.
* How CPU, RAM, storage, and I/O work together.
* How a CPU executes instructions.
* What registers, caches, RAM, and storage actually do.
* How programs become executable processes.
* How the operating system manages hardware.
* What kernels and system calls are.
* How processes and threads work.
* How memory is managed.
* How filesystems organize persistent data.
* How computers boot.
* Why virtualization exists.
* What a virtual machine actually is.
* What a server really is.
* Why Linux became dominant in server environments.
* Why containers can exist on top of operating-system primitives.
* Why these concepts matter for Docker, Kubernetes, cloud, databases, and AI workloads.

---

# 🧠 Learning Principle

Do not memorize:

> "RAM is temporary memory."

Instead understand:

```text
CPU needs data
      ↓
Registers / Cache
      ↓
RAM
      ↓
Storage
```

Ask:

* Why are there multiple levels?
* Why isn't everything RAM?
* Why isn't everything storage?
* Why is cache faster?
* What happens when the needed data isn't available?
* What does the operating system do?

The goal is to understand the **reasoning behind the architecture**.

```text
Hardware
```

And you should understand what is happening at each major layer.

That mental model will become one of the foundations of everything that follows.
