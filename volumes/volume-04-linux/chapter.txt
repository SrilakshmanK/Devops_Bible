📘 Volume 04 — Linux
🎯 Objective

Develop a deep understanding of Linux as an operating system and become comfortable administering Linux systems entirely from the command line.

Outcome:
After completing this volume, you should be able to confidently navigate, configure, troubleshoot, secure, and maintain a Linux system—the foundation of almost every DevOps environment.

Prerequisites for Volume 05

Before moving on, you should be able to:

Navigate Linux confidently without a GUI.
Manage files, users, permissions, services, and packages.
Configure networking and SSH.
Analyze logs and troubleshoot common issues.
Monitor system resources.
Perform routine Linux administration tasks.
Understand enough Linux internals to automate them with Bash.
