📘 Volume 16 — Observability & Monitoring
🎯 Objective

Master observability by collecting, visualizing, analyzing, and alerting on metrics, logs, and traces from applications and infrastructure.

Outcome:
After completing this volume, you should be able to monitor production systems, troubleshoot failures quickly, create dashboards, configure alerts, and build a complete observability platform.
