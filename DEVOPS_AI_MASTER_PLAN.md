# Non-Negotiable Roadmap Rules

1. Every volume must teach fundamentals before advanced concepts.
2. Every volume must contain practical work.
3. Every volume must have a meaningful mini-project or equivalent practical deliverable.
4. Mini-projects are selected by the roadmap, not by the learner.
5. Chapter exercises are smaller than volume projects.
6. Do not create unnecessary projects just to satisfy a "project per volume" rule.
7. From Volume 12 onward, the flagship is the primary practical environment whenever appropriate.
8. The flagship is NOT a volume.
9. The flagship is selected only after Volume 11.
10. The flagship is designed in detail before implementation.
11. The flagship progressively evolves through Docker → CI/CD → AWS → Terraform → Kubernetes → Security → Observability → LLMOps → SRE.
12. Database Engineering remains a separate deep-learning track.
13. DSA remains a separate problem-solving track.
14. Do not introduce advanced technologies before their prerequisites.
15. Do not skip fundamentals simply because they are not immediately needed by the flagship.
16. Each volume must have clear completion criteria.
17. Interview preparation is the final consolidation stage, not something postponed until the last week.
18. The roadmap should prioritize understanding over technology collection.
19. The roadmap should prioritize building over passive course consumption.
20. If the roadmap needs to change, update this master structure first so it remains the source of truth.
